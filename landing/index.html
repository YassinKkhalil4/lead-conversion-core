  3. 03

    Nine questions, then a score you can audit

    Permission, location, unit type, budget, payment plan, down payment, timeline, purpose, site visit. Ten weighted factors make up the score, and each one carries a written reason.

    The score is arithmetic. The same answers always give the same number, so a sales manager can check why a lead ranked where it did, today or next month. See all ten factors and their points.

What brokers ask first.

Is this a chatbot?

No. A chatbot writes its own replies. Kadensio sends the replies you approved, in the order you set, and scores the answers with fixed arithmetic. It contains no generative model, so it cannot make up a price, a payment plan or a unit.

What does it cost?

Kadensio has no published price. You agree any subscription fee in writing. Meta bills its messaging fees at cost and Kadensio adds no markup. The Terms of Service set this out in full.

What if a buyer's answer doesn't fit the question?

It asks once more. If the second answer still doesn't fit, it saves the buyer's own words as a note for your salesperson and moves on to the next question. Your salesperson still sees what the buyer wrote.

Can a salesperson step in?

At any point. Taking over stops the engine replying to that buyer until someone hands the conversation back.

What happens when a buyer says stop?

The engine opts them out on the spot, stops messaging them and marks the lead as not interested.

Does it work with the number we already use?

It runs on the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform, so you register the number there. If the number your buyers know is on the ordinary WhatsApp Business app today, moving it is the first thing we go through together.

Where is our data kept?

On servers in Frankfurt, Germany, with backups in the same region. Meta handles the messages it delivers, and Google handles calendar events if you connect a calendar. The Data Processing Addendum lists every sub-processor.

Which languages does it speak?

Arabic and English ship, both tested against the production API. For any other language your buyers use, we write and load it during setup, the same way we add a custom question.
