How it works

Four steps, no one on shift.

  1. 01

    They message your number

    A number on a listing, a QR code on a hoarding, a referral forwarding your contact. A stranger messaging you cold is the main way leads arrive — and the one nothing else answers. Website forms and Facebook lead ads feed in as well.

    • WhatsApp, direct — primary
    • Website form
    • Facebook lead ad

  2. 02

    It answers in seconds, in their language

    The lead picks Arabic or English before anything else; nothing is guessed from the number. Both ship and are verified against the production API. Any other language your buyers use is written and loaded during setup.

  3. 03

    Nine questions, then a score that shows its work

    Permission, location, unit type, budget, payment plan, down payment, timeline, purpose, site visit. Ten weighted factors produce the score, each carrying a written reason, so a sales manager can read exactly why a lead ranked where it did.

  4. 04

    Routed to the right desk, and booked

    The lead goes to the salesperson whose project coverage matches, not to whoever is free. If they want to see the unit, Kadensio offers real slots and writes the appointment to your Google Calendar. Unacknowledged, it reminds at fifteen minutes and escalates at thirty.
