The problem

A lead goes cold in the time it takes to notice them.

A buyer sees a listing, taps through to WhatsApp, and messages — at eleven at night, from Moscow, Mumbai or Manchester. Property Finder, Bayut, whatever you run in‑house: your CRM will record that they arrived, assign them, and report on them. It will not ask them their budget. By the time someone opens the tab, the viewing has been booked with whoever replied first.

  • 15 min Reminder to the assigned salesperson if the lead has not been acknowledged.
  • 30 min Escalation. The clock runs unattended, including outside working hours.

The cost of slow response

You have already paid for the lead.

The spend is gone the moment the message arrives. Everything after that is a question of how long it sits there unanswered. What happens in the first five minutes decides whether that money becomes a conversation, or a row in a sheet nobody opens again. The larger the ticket, the more each missed reply costs — and in Dubai the tickets are large.

How it works

Five steps, start to finish.

No app for the lead to install, no form to chase, no one on shift.

  1. 01

    The lead arrives

    Three ways in. Direct WhatsApp is the main path — the broker puts the number on a listing and it works from there.

    • WhatsApp, direct
    • Website form
    • Facebook lead ad

  2. 02

    Reply within seconds, in their language

    Kadensio responds immediately and asks the lead which language they want. Arabic and English ship with the product; any other language your buyers use is written and loaded during setup, the same way custom questions are. Nothing is guessed from the number or the phrasing — the lead picks, and the whole conversation follows.

  3. 03

    Nine questions

    A fixed qualification, asked one at a time, the way a good agent would ask them.

    • Permission
    • Location
    • Unit type
    • Budget
    • Payment plan
    • Down payment
    • Timeline
    • Purpose
    • Site visit interest

  4. 04

    Scored, then routed

    Ten weighted factors produce a score, and every factor carries a written reason. The lead goes to the salesperson whose project coverage matches — not to whoever happens to be free.

  5. 05

    Booked, or escalated

    If the lead wants to see the unit, Kadensio offers real time slots and books one. If a salesperson has not picked the lead up, the reminder fires at fifteen minutes and the escalation at thirty.

Why it's different

Three decisions that shaped the product.

The score is deterministic, and it shows its work

Ten weighted factors, each producing a stated reason. The same answers always produce the same score, and a sales manager can read exactly why a lead ranked where it did. Nothing about the ranking is a judgement call you cannot inspect.

Language is configuration, not code

Dubai buyers are Russian, Indian, British, Chinese, Gulf Arab. Kadensio ships bilingual — Arabic and English, both verified end to end against the production API — and the lead picks before qualification begins. Any further language is written and loaded during setup, the same way custom questions are.

WhatsApp‑native, not a widget on a page

This runs on the number a buyer already messaged, in the app already open on their phone. No web chat bubble waiting to be ignored, no login, no download — the conversation happens where the lead started it.

Configuration

The nine questions are a starting point, not a script.

Every desk qualifies differently. Change the wording, reorder them, drop the ones you never ask, and add the ones you actually ask on a call. In Arabic and English, or in whichever language you have loaded during setup. The engine underneath does not change — what changes is that the qualification sounds like your brokerage instead of like software.

Where this fits

Built for Dubai. Not limited to it.

Across the Gulf, North Africa, South Asia, and much of Latin America and Southern Europe, WhatsApp is not a support channel — it is the sales channel. It is where a buyer first reaches a broker, asks what else is available, and agrees to a viewing. Lead tools built around email sequences and website chat widgets were designed for a market that does not work this way.
