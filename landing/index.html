How it works

Four steps, with nobody on shift.

  1. 01

    They message your number

    A number on a listing, a QR code on a hoarding, a contact a friend forwarded. Cold WhatsApp messages are how most property leads arrive, and nothing else answers them. Website forms and Facebook lead ads feed in too.

    • WhatsApp, direct — primary
    • Website form
    • Facebook lead ad

  2. 02

    It replies in seconds, in their language

    The buyer picks Arabic or English before anything else. Nothing is guessed from the phone number. Both languages ship and are verified against the production API; any other is written and loaded during setup.

  3. 03

    Nine questions, then a score you can audit

    Permission, location, unit type, budget, payment plan, down payment, timeline, purpose, site visit. Ten weighted factors make up the score, and each one carries a written reason.

    The score is arithmetic, not judgement. The same answers always give the same number, so a sales manager can see exactly why a lead ranked where it did, and get the same answer tomorrow. See all ten factors and their points.

  4. 04

    Sent to the right desk, and booked

    The lead goes to the salesperson who covers that project, not to whoever happens to be free. If the buyer wants a viewing, Kadensio offers real slots and writes the booking to your Google Calendar. If nobody acknowledges the lead, it sends a reminder at 15 minutes and alerts the manager at 30.

Questions

What brokers ask first.

Is this a chatbot?

No. A chatbot writes its own replies. Kadensio sends the replies you approved, in the order you set, and scores the answers with fixed arithmetic. There is no generative model anywhere in it, so it cannot make up a price, a payment plan or a unit.

What if a buyer's answer doesn't fit the question?

It asks once more. If the second answer still doesn't fit, it saves the buyer's own words as a note for your salesperson and moves on to the next question. Nothing the buyer writes is thrown away.

Can a salesperson step in?

At any point. Taking over stops the engine replying to that buyer until someone hands the conversation back.

What happens when a buyer says stop?

They are opted out on the spot. The engine stops messaging them and the lead is marked as not interested.

Does it work with the number we already use?

It runs on the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform, so the number has to be registered there. If the number your buyers know is on the ordinary WhatsApp Business app today, moving it is the first thing we go through together.

Where is our data kept?

On servers in Frankfurt, Germany, with backups in the same region. Meta handles the messages it delivers, and Google handles calendar events if you connect a calendar. The Data Processing Addendum lists every sub-processor.

Which languages does it speak?

Arabic and English ship, and both are verified against the production API. Any other language your buyers use is written and loaded during setup, the same way a custom question is.
